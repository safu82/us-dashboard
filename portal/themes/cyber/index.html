Cybersecurity

The layers of digital defense — from who gets in (identity) through the devices, network, cloud, data and code, up to the security control room and the people who run it.

Deep Tech · 9 stages · closes to 2026-11-20

The Cybersecurity value chain has 9 stages, in order: Identity & Access, Endpoint Protection, Network & Firewall, Cloud & App Security, Email & Web Security, Data Security & Backup, Vulnerability & Code Security, Detection & Response (SOC), Security Services & Consulting. 37 companies are mapped across them, and at the close on 2026-11-20 the strongest stage by momentum is Email & Web Security at 47 out of 100.

A value chain is the order in which value is actually added, from raw inputs through to whoever sells the finished thing. Reading a theme this way shows where in it the money is moving, which is rarely evenly spread. Each stage carries its own momentum score and the companies we map to it. The mapping is ours and deliberately hand-made; it will not match any index.

Value is added along that order, from raw inputs through to whoever sells the finished thing.

Which companies are in the Cybersecurity value chain?

37 companies are mapped across the 9 stages, each scored against every US stock we rank rather than only against the others here. Every stage and its companies are listed on this page, at the close on 2026-11-20.

Which part of the Cybersecurity value chain has the strongest momentum?

Email & Web Security, at 47 out of 100 at the close on 2026-11-20. Momentum is cross-sectional — it ranks these companies against the rest of the US market — so in a falling market the leading stage is the one that fell least, not one that rose.

Does the Cybersecurity value chain match an index or an ETF?

No. The mapping is ours and hand-made: 37 companies placed at 9 stages by a judgement about what belongs where. It matches no index and no fund’s holdings, and it will sometimes be wrong or out of date.
