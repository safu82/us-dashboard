Food & Agriculture

The farm-to-table supply chain and the food-security story behind it — from the seeds, fertilizer and machinery that grow the crop, through animal health and the processors & ingredient makers, to the distributors that get food to the table. Spans materials, industrials, health care and staples.

A value chain is the order in which value is actually added, from raw inputs through to whoever sells the finished thing. Reading a theme this way shows where in it the money is moving, which is rarely evenly spread. Each stage carries its own momentum score and the companies we map to it. Which part of the Food & Agriculture value chain has the strongest momentum?
