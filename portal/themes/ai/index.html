Artificial Intelligence

The end-to-end AI value chain — from the materials and tools that make chips, through compute, memory, networking, power and cooling, up to the clouds and software that sell AI.

Deep Tech · 16 stages · closes to 2026-10-23

A value chain is the order in which value is actually added, from raw inputs through to whoever sells the finished thing. Reading a theme this way shows where in it the money is moving, which is rarely evenly spread. Each stage carries its own momentum score and the companies we map to it. How to read the numbers. Momentum is a 0–100 score: each name ranked against its own peer group on relative-strength level and trend, 9- and 20-day EMA slopes, and price against its 50-day average. 50 is the median, not a neutral return — in a falling market a high score means falling less than the rest. Funds are scored against other funds and stocks against other stocks, so the two are not comparable with each other. RS rank runs the other way: #1 is the strongest stock we cover.
