Quantum Computing

The race to build computers that harness quantum physics — from the picks-and-shovels hardware, through the pure-play startups and big-tech giants building the machines, to the "quantum-safe" security layer rising to defend against them and the industries set to benefit first.

The Quantum Computing value chain has 5 stages, in order: Enabling Hardware, Big Tech & Cloud, Pure-Play Quantum, Quantum-Safe Security, Who Benefits First. 19 companies are mapped across them, and at the close on 2026-11-18 the strongest stage by momentum is Big Tech & Cloud at 40 out of 100.

A value chain is the order in which value is actually added, from raw inputs through to whoever sells the finished thing. Reading a theme this way shows where in it the money is moving, which is rarely evenly spread. Each stage carries its own momentum score and the companies we map to it.
