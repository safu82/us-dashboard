Reshoring & US Manufacturing

The multi-year push to rebuild US factory capacity — from the raw materials and the firms that construct the plants, through the automation and electrical gear that outfit them, to the rail and warehousing that moves the output. A policy-driven story cutting across industrials, materials and real estate.

A value chain is the order in which value is actually added, from raw inputs through to whoever sells the finished thing. Reading a theme this way shows where in it the money is moving, which is rarely evenly spread. Each stage carries its own momentum score and the companies we map to it.
