Housing & Homebuilders

The US housing machine, end to end — from the building products that go into a home, through the homebuilders that put them up and the mortgage lenders that finance the buyers, to the retailers where owners renovate and the REITs that rent it all out. A classic rate-cut play.

Real Economy · 5 stages · closes to 2026-10-30

A value chain is the order in which value is actually added, from raw inputs through to whoever sells the finished thing. Reading a theme this way shows where in it the money is moving, which is rarely evenly spread. Each stage carries its own momentum score and the companies we map to it. The mapping is ours and deliberately hand-made; it will not match any index.
