Housing & Homebuilders

The US housing machine, end to end — from the building products that go into a home, through the homebuilders that put them up and the mortgage lenders that finance the buyers, to the retailers where owners renovate and the REITs that rent it all out. A classic rate-cut play.

Real Economy · 5 stages · closes to 2026-11-19

The Housing & Homebuilders value chain has 5 stages, in order: Building Products & Materials, Homebuilders, Mortgage & Title, Residential REITs & Rental, Home Improvement Retail. 19 companies are mapped across them, and at the close on 2026-11-19 the strongest stage by momentum is Home Improvement Retail at 78 out of 100.

A value chain is the order in which value is actually added, from raw inputs through to whoever sells the finished thing. Reading a theme this way shows where in it the money is moving, which is rarely evenly spread. Each stage carries its own momentum score and the companies we map to it. The mapping is ours and deliberately hand-made; it will not match any index.

Questions about this value chain

What are the stages of the Housing & Homebuilders value chain?

5 stages, in order: Building Products & Materials, Homebuilders, Mortgage & Title, Residential REITs & Rental, Home Improvement Retail. Value is added along that order, from raw inputs through to whoever sells the finished thing.

Which companies are in the Housing & Homebuilders value chain?

19 companies are mapped across the 5 stages, each scored against every US stock we rank rather than only against the others here. Every stage and its companies are listed on this page, at the close on 2026-11-19.

Which part of the Housing & Homebuilders value chain has the strongest momentum?

Home Improvement Retail, at 78 out of 100 at the close on 2026-11-19. Momentum is cross-sectional — it ranks these companies against the rest of the US market — so in a falling market the leading stage is the one that fell least, not one that rose.

Does the Housing & Homebuilders value chain match an index or an ETF?

No. The mapping is ours and hand-made: 19 companies placed at 5 stages by a judgement about what belongs where. It matches no index and no fund’s holdings, and it will sometimes be wrong or out of date.
