How to read the numbers. Momentum is a 0–100 score: each name ranked against its own peer group on relative-strength level and trend, 9- and 20-day EMA slopes, and price against its 50-day average. 50 is the median, not a neutral return — in a falling market a high score means falling less than the rest. Funds are scored against other funds and stocks against other stocks, so the two are not comparable with each other. RS rank runs the other way: #1 is the strongest stock we cover.
