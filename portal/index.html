How to read this. Momentum is a 0-100 score: each name is ranked against the whole ~1,900-stock US universe on relative-strength level and trend, 9- and 20-day EMA slopes, and price against its 50-day average. 50 is the median stock, not a neutral return — in a falling market a high score means falling less than everything else. ETFs are scored the same way but against other ETFs, so the two numbers are not comparable with each other.

RS rank is a position, not a score, and it runs the other way: #1 is the strongest stock in the market, so lower is better. It ranks a stock's price against the S&P 500 over the past few weeks, so #15 / 1,934 means only fourteen US stocks we cover were performing better. A name can hold a high momentum score and a middling RS rank at the same time — momentum rewards a trend that is improving, RS rewards one that is already ahead.

Durability is the share of the last 60 trading days a fund spent in the top 10% of the board; the average fund scores about 10%. 8/21 means the price closed above its 8-day EMA with the 8 above the 21.

Data is rebuilt each weeknight after the US close and is delayed, not live. Value chains are our own mapping and will not match any index or fund's holdings.
